# Research Context

This context names research workflows and research outputs.

## Language

**Deep Research**:
A multi-step workflow that finds sources, reads them, and makes a research report.
_Avoid_: Web search, quick answer

**Research Job**:
One Deep Research run.
_Avoid_: Search query

**Source**:
Material used by a research job, such as a web page, document, or provider record.
_Avoid_: Evidence when it has not been used to support a claim

**Finding**:
Something learned from sources during research.
_Avoid_: Knowledge unless saved

**Report**:
A structured research output with sections, combined findings, and evidence you can trace to its source.
_Avoid_: Summary, quick answer
